About Me

Design is how I give life to words

I'm Toba — a Lagos-based freelance Content & Social Media Designer. My design journey started at a photo lab during secondary school, and that hands-on foundation is still at the core of everything I create.

I work across Photoshop and After Effects, building visuals for food brands, churches, events, and businesses that want their message to actually land. Every design I make has purpose — not just pixels.
